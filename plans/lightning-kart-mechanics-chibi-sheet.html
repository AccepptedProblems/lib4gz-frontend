Lightning Kart chibi sheet

A proposal, drawn with the same parts the game draws from. The driver is the four playable characters with today's colours; Splash is the new character who shoots the bubble and throws the bottle. Nothing here is final until you say so.

Behind view, on the road

After the reference sprites: the head two thirds of the figure, a rounded square wider than tall, heavy brows, a small mouth, cel shading, goggles on the helmet's brim, white gloves on the wheel. Bolt shows the streak badge at tier Charged. Each kart is drawn at the size it has just behind the camera.

Portraits, for the Garage and the Ready board

Splash, the bubble and the bottle

Splash slides in from the right edge. On a wrong answer she fires the bubble; on a right one she throws the bottle. The bottle is also the nitro cell's icon in the HUD.
