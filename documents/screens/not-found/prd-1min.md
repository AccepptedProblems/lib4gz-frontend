# Not Found (404) — PRD 1-min

- **Problem.** Users who hit a broken or stale link land on a blank route and have no way forward.
- **User group.** Anyone hitting an unknown URL — authenticated or not.
- **Outcome.** A short, friendly page that confirms the URL is wrong and offers one clear way back.
- **Success metric.** Bounce rate on `**` route drops; > 80% of 404 visits end with a click on the primary CTA.
- **In scope.** Static page, primary CTA (dashboard or login), back link.
- **Out of scope.** Suggested-pages search, support form, telemetry on referrer (later).

See `prd-5min.md`, `prd-full.md`.
